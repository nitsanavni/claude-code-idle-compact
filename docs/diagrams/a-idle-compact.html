Twenty quiet minutes, then a compact

When a turn ends, the mod arms a 20-minute timer. Nobody comes back, the timer fires, the context is still at least 120k tokens, so the conversation is compacted before the prompt cache goes cold.

1 column = 1 minute; the ≈ break skips ten quiet minutes. idleSeconds (1200) and minTokens (120000) are live-tunable.

How to read this diagram

Each row is a lane: the timeline of one actor (claude, timer, compact), read left to right. The numbers along the top mark elapsed time; the faint dotted lines let you line moments up across lanes.

A dot is a moment, something that happens at one instant:

  • turn.start
  • turn.complete — arms the timer
  • timer fires — context ≥ 120k
  • compact — fresh, small context

A soft capsule is a span, something that lasts. Its length is its duration:

  • turn
  • idle timer · 20 min
  • Time break — the timeline skips ahead here (+10 min); time is not to scale across it, so a span that crosses it lasts longer than it looks.
  • Thin line — time passing with nothing happening. A gap in a lane means that actor is inactive.
