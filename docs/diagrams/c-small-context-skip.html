A small context is left alone

The countdown runs out as usual, but the conversation holds only 64k tokens, under the 120k minimum. Re-reading something that small is cheap, so idle-compact leaves it exactly as it was.

1 column = 1 minute. The minimum is the minTokens setting; set it to 0 to always compact.

How to read this diagram

Each row is a lane: the timeline of one actor (Claude, Countdown, Compaction), read left to right. The numbers along the top mark elapsed time; the faint vertical lines let you line moments up across lanes.

A marble (a round dot) is a moment, something that happens at one instant. Each marble carries a letter; this is what the letters stand for:

  • message sent — you ask, Claude starts
  • reply done — the countdown starts
  • time is up — only 64k tokens
  • left alone — 64k is under the 120k minimum

A pill is a span, something that lasts. Its length is its duration:

  • Claude working
  • idle countdown · 20 min
  • Thin line — time passing with nothing happening. A gap in a lane means that actor is inactive.

The Detail buttons above the figure show less or more: Text is the diagram typed out in plain characters, the way its source is written (- a lane, ● a marble, bold = something that lasts, ≈ a time break), Schematic is the same thing drawn, still with no words, where something that lasts is just a bolder stretch of its lane, Simple adds the lane names and a letter in each marble, More adds each marble's name and the time scale, and Full adds a line of detail under each name.

The words used here:

  • Context — everything Claude reads to answer you: the whole conversation so far.
  • Tokens — the units a conversation is measured in; a token is roughly three quarters of a word. 64k is 64,000 tokens.
