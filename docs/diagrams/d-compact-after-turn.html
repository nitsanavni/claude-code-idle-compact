Compacting on request, right after the turn

You can ask Claude to compact without waiting. Claude passes the request to idle-compact, which lets the current reply finish, waits one second, then compacts: no 20-minute wait and no size check.

How to read this diagram

Each row is a lane: the timeline of one actor (Claude, Request, Wait, Compaction), read left to right. The numbers along the top mark elapsed time; the faint vertical lines let you line moments up across lanes.

A marble (a round dot) is a moment, something that happens at one instant. Each marble carries a letter; this is what the letters stand for:

  • message sent — you ask, Claude starts
  • reply done
  • compact requested — Claude uses compact_after_turn
  • wait over — 1 second later
  • compacted — now a short summary

A pill is a span, something that lasts. Its length is its duration:

  • Claude working
  • 1 s wait
  • Thin line — time passing with nothing happening. A gap in a lane means that actor is inactive.

The Detail buttons above the figure are five ready-made views, from Text (the diagram typed out in plain characters, the way its source is written: - a lane, ● a marble, bold = something that lasts, ≈ a time break) through Schematic (the same thing drawn, with no words) to Full. The Show switches under them turn single parts on and off, and the spacing adjusts to what is shown. The page address keeps the view, so you can link to it.

The words used here:

  • Turn — one round of the conversation: your message, and everything Claude does until its reply is done.
  • compact_after_turn — the tool idle-compact gives Claude for this. You just say "compact after this turn".
