Compacting on request, right after the turn

Claude can ask for a compact itself with the compact_after_turn tool. The mod waits for the turn to finish, gives it a one-second beat, then compacts, with no idle wait and no token threshold.

1 column = 0.25 s. The one-second delay lets the turn settle before /compact runs.

How to read this diagram

Each row is a lane: the timeline of one actor (claude, tool, clock, compact), read left to right. The numbers along the top mark elapsed time; the faint dotted lines let you line moments up across lanes.

A dot is a moment, something that happens at one instant:

  • turn.start
  • turn.complete
  • compact_after_turn — tool call
  • fires — 1 s later
  • compact

A soft capsule is a span, something that lasts. Its length is its duration:

  • turn
  • 1 s — clock.after(1000)
  • Thin line — time passing with nothing happening. A gap in a lane means that actor is inactive.
