Compacting on request, right after the turn

Claude can ask for a compact itself with the compact_after_turn tool. The mod waits for the turn to finish, gives it a one-second beat, then compacts, with no idle wait and no token threshold.

How to read this diagram

Each row is a lane: the timeline of one actor (Claude, Tool, Clock, Compact), read left to right. The numbers along the top mark elapsed time; the faint vertical lines let you line moments up across lanes.

A marble is a moment, something that happens at one instant. The letter in it is its symbol in the ASCII source:

A pill is a span, something that lasts. Its length is its duration:
